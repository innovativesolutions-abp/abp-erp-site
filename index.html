GBrain & AI-assisted workflows

Not just more data.
Useful knowledge.

GBrain is the platform’s knowledge capability: a way to preserve authorized context and retrieve it when it matters. AI-assisted workflows can help with tasks and understanding information; their use depends on the enabled feature and its configuration.
